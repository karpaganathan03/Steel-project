Zero Secondary Scrap

Authenticated MTC Dossier

Every dispatch includes authentic manufacturer Mill Test Certificates indicating heat numbers, carbon-sulphur limits, and 180° bend test results.

IS 1786 & IS 2062 Certified

Transparent Direct Pricing

Itemized commercial proposals indexing base rates, freight, and GST. Zero unverified surcharges or surprise unloading fees at the site gate.
