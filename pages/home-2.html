How do I request an accurate steel quote?

Share the product, grade, diameter or section, approximate quantity, required documentation and delivery location. Attach or reference your material schedule where applicable.

Which TMT grade should I choose?

Use the grade specified in your approved structural design. Consult your qualified project engineer before selecting or substituting a grade.

Are all listed products in stock?

No. Product listings are indicative. Confirm current stock, quantities, lengths, certification and delivery options with the team before ordering.

Can you provide test certificates?

Available documents depend on manufacturer, product and batch. Request the applicable certificates or test records during quotation and verify them against your project requirements.
