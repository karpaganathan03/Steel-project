Specification notes

Read the product standard, not just the grade name

IS 1786 covers high-strength deformed steel bars and wires for concrete reinforcement. Verify the current applicable edition, grade, dimensions, tolerances, chemical and mechanical requirements, and manufacturer test documentation for every order.

  • Follow the grade and bar size in the approved structural drawings.
  • Check manufacturer markings and batch-specific test certificates.
  • Ask the project engineer before accepting any grade substitution.

The information on this page is general guidance, not engineering advice or a certification of supplied products.
