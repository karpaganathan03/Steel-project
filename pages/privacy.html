1. Information We Collect

When you interact with ForgeLine Steel (via our online price enquiry forms, structural rebar calculators, WhatsApp channel, or phone dispatch desk), we collect relevant procurement information necessary to quote and fulfill orders:

  • Contact Particulars: Your name, company or contracting firm name, phone number, email address, and job site delivery location.
  • Steel Specifications: Rebar grades (Fe 500D, Fe 550D, Fe 500S), nominal diameters (8 mm to 32 mm), tonnage requirements, and Bar Bending Schedules (BBS).
  • Delivery Logistics: Site accessibility constraints, trailer or crane offloading requirements, and requested dispatch timelines.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your submitted details exclusively for commercial and operational purposes:

  • Preparing accurate, transparent commercial quotations and freight estimates.
  • Coordinating dispatches from our Virudhunagar stockyard or direct primary rolling mills (SAIL, Tata Tiscon, JSW Neosteel, Jindal Panther).
  • Issuing certified Mill Test Certificates (MTC) and dual-weighbridge gross-tare-net printouts.
  • Responding to technical metallurgy inquiries from structural consultants and civil engineers.

3. Local Browser Storage & Cookies

This web portal utilizes local browser storage (HTML5 LocalStorage and SessionStorage) to store your preferred interface theme (Light or Dark), layout direction (LTR or RTL), and draft enquiry calculations. No tracking or marketing cookies are installed without explicit opt-in.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

ForgeLine Steel does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or commercial procurement data to third-party advertisers. Data is shared strictly with authorized logistics carriers and primary rolling mills on a need-to-know basis solely to accomplish vehicle weighment, gate-pass entry, and offloading at your destination.

5. Data Retention & Security

Procurement documents, invoices, and digital weighbridge receipts are maintained for statutory periods mandated by commercial tax laws in India. All digital transmissions are secured through modern cryptographic protocols (TLS/SSL).
