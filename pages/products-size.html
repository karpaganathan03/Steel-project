Nominal diameter Approx. mass per metre Approx. 12 m bars per tonne Reference length

Theoretical mass is estimated using d²/162 kg/m. Bar counts are calculated from that estimate and a 12 m length, then rounded to whole bars. Actual mass and supplied lengths can vary; confirm product documentation and order quantities with the supplier.
