Frequently Asked Questions

Steel Supply & Service FAQs

Common questions on order quantities, MTC documentation, custom BBS fabrication, and site logistics.

01 What is the minimum order quantity for direct site dispatch?

We supply steel for all project scales. For individual residential projects, we deliver loose bundles starting from 1 Metric Ton (MT). For commercial, infrastructure, and industrial developments, we supply full trailer loads (15 MT to 35 MT) directly from primary mills with computerized weighbridge slips.

02 Are original Mill Test Certificates (MTC) supplied with every batch?

Yes. Every single dispatch is accompanied by an authenticated manufacturer Mill Test Certificate (MTC) indicating chemical analysis (Carbon, Sulphur, and Phosphorus percentages) and physical properties (0.2% Proof Stress, Tensile Strength, Elongation, and 180° Bend/Re-bend tests) matching IS 1786 or IS 2062 standards.

03 Can you supply custom cut-to-length and pre-bent rebar (BBS)?

Yes. Our automated off-site cut-and-bend facility processes your structural consultant's Bar Bending Schedule (BBS) to fabricate pre-cut rebar, stirrups, column cages, and foundation mats to exact tolerances. This minimizes on-site scrap from typical 8-12% down to near zero and speeds site placement by up to 30%.

04 How do you verify weight accuracy during dispatch?

Every dispatch vehicle undergoes a dual computerized gross-tare-net weighing process on our calibrated, stamp-verified electronic weighbridges. A stamped printout indicating truck registration, entry/exit timestamp, gross weight, tare weight, and net steel tonnage accompanies your delivery challan and invoice.

05 Can deliveries be phased according to our pouring schedule?

Yes. We coordinate staged delivery schedules aligned precisely with your site pouring calendar (substructure, podium, columns, and slabs). This ensures you have reinforcement ready on-site without cluttering limited staging space or exposing steel to prolonged weather corrosion.

06 Which primary mills do you supply from, and can orders combine products?

We are authorized distributors and channel partners for India's leading primary steel manufacturers, including Tata Tiscon, JSW Neosteel, SAIL, and Jindal Steel. We can combine multiple diameters of TMT rebar, structural beams, MS channels, and binding wire in a single coordinated project consignment.
