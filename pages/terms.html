Effective Date: November 1, 2026 | Applicability: All steel quotations, dispatches, and supply agreements issued by ForgeLine Steel & TMT.

1. Quotations & Daily Price Fluctuations

Because primary steel billets and raw materials are actively traded on national commodities markets, all commercial price quotations issued by ForgeLine Steel are valid for the specific timeframe stated on the official quotation sheet (typically 24 to 48 hours). Final billing prices are locked upon advance payment confirmation and purchase order issuance.

2. Quality Standards & Tolerances

All high-tensile reinforcement bars (Fe 415, Fe 500, Fe 500D, Fe 550D, Fe 600) adhere strictly to Bureau of Indian Standards benchmark IS 1786:2008:

  • Weight Tolerance: Permissible nominal weight tolerances per meter (+/- 7% for ≤10mm, +/- 5% for 12–16mm, +/- 3% for ≥20mm) conform to BIS standards.
  • Length: Standard bar bundles are supplied at 12.0 meters standard length with factory rolling tolerance.
  • Mill Test Certificates (MTC): Original batch test certificates demonstrating physical yield strength, chemical composition, and elongation parameters accompany each dispatch invoice.

3. Dual Digital Weighbridge Dispatch

Every transport vehicle undergoes dual digital weighing (gross and tare) at our certified electronic weighbridges in Virudhunagar prior to gate exit. An unalterable digital weighment slip stating tare weight, gross weight, net tonnage, date, timestamp, and lorry registration number forms the binding basis for commercial invoicing.

4. Site Delivery & Offloading Responsibilities

Unless an all-inclusive offloading package is explicitly contracted in writing, the buyer is responsible for providing safe, clear vehicular access to the unloading area and suitable mechanical equipment (crane, forklift, or unloading crew) to offload material within standard vehicular detention limits (maximum 3 hours from arrival).

5. Inspection, Claims & Returns

The buyer's site engineer or authorized representative must verify bundle seals, brand stamp markings, and physical condition upon arrival before signing the dispatch manifest. Any claims regarding transit damage, seal tampering, or bundle discrepancy must be recorded on the delivery challan and notified to ForgeLine Steel within 24 hours of delivery.

6. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

All commercial agreements, supply contracts, and disputes arising from steel supply or quotation with ForgeLine Steel are governed exclusively by the laws of India and subject to the jurisdiction of the competent courts in Virudhunagar, Tamil Nadu.
