IS 1786 Steel Grade Comparison Guide

Mechanical benchmarks and characteristics of primary reinforcement steel grades to guide your project engineering team.

Grade Designation Min Yield Strength (fy) Ultimate Tensile Strength Min Elongation (%) Seismic & Bend Rating Ideal Application
Fe 500 500 N/mm² (MPa) 545 N/mm² 12.0% Standard Ductility Low-rise residential, boundary walls, minor slab casting
Fe 500D 500 N/mm² (MPa) 565 N/mm² 16.0% High Seismic Ductility (Class D) Multi-story apartments, earthquake zones III, IV & V, bridges
Fe 550D 550 N/mm² (MPa) 600 N/mm² 14.5% Superior Strength + High Ductility Commercial towers, metro flyovers, heavy industrial frames
Fe 600 600 N/mm² (MPa) 660 N/mm² 10.0% Ultra-High Tensile Strength Mega expressways, marine ports, deep foundation piles
CRS (Corrosion Resistant) 500 / 550 N/mm² 565 / 600 N/mm² 16.0% Anti-Corrosive Copper & Chrome Alloyed Coastal constructions, marine structures, effluent plants

* Values conform to Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications IS 1786:2008. Verify certified Mill Test Certificates (MTC) supplied with every ForgeLine dispatch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Procuring Steel with ForgeLine

Common questions from civil contractors, architects, and independent home builders.

What is the minimum order quantity for direct site dispatch?

We supply steel for all project scales. For individual residential projects, we deliver loose bundles (minimum 1 MT). For commercial and infrastructure sites, we supply full trailer loads (15 MT to 35 MT) directly from primary mills with computerized weighbridge slips.

Are original Mill Test Certificates (MTC) supplied with every batch?

Yes. Every dispatch is accompanied by an authenticated manufacturer Mill Test Certificate (MTC) indicating chemical analysis (Carbon, Sulphur, Phosphorus percentages) and physical properties (Yield Strength, Tensile Strength, Elongation, and 180° Bend test).

How do you verify weight accuracy during dispatch?

Every dispatch vehicle undergoes a dual computerized gross-tare-net weighing process on our calibrated digital weighbridges. A stamped printout with time, truck registration, and certified weight accompanies your delivery invoice.

Can you supply custom cut-to-length and pre-bent rebar (BBS)?

Yes. Our automated off-site fabrication center can process your structural consultant's Bar Bending Schedule (BBS) to provide factory-bent stirrups, column cages, and cut-to-length bars, reducing on-site scrap and labor by up to 12%.
